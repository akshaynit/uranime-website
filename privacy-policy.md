# Privacy Policy for urAnime

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

urAnime ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the urAnime mobile application.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

- **Account information:** Email address and authentication details when you create an account.
- **Profile information:** Username, display name, profile picture, and information you choose to add to your profile.
- **App activity:** Anime-related content you choose to track, such as your watchlist, ratings, and preferences.
- **User-generated content:** Information you share through social features, such as comments, reviews, or profile sharing.
- **Technical information:** Device and app information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the app.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and maintain urAnime's features.
- Manage your account and authenticate you.
- Save your anime-related preferences and activity.
- Enable social and profile-sharing features.
- Improve app functionality and security.
- Respond to support requests.

## Third-Party Services

urAnime uses third-party services to operate certain features. These may include:

- **Supabase:** Account authentication, database storage, and backend services.
- **Resend:** Sending account-related emails.

These providers process information as necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Sharing and Visibility

Information you choose to share through profile or social features may be visible to other users according to the app's sharing settings.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the app's services, maintain account functionality, and meet applicable legal obligations.
