# Privacy Policy for urAnime

**Last updated: October 29, 2026**

urAnime (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the urAnime mobile application.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

- **Account information:** Email address and authentication information when you create an account or sign in.
- **Profile information:** Username, display name, profile picture, and information you choose to add to your profile.
- **Anime library information:** Anime you track, watch status, episode progress, ratings, and preferences.
- **Memories and journal content:** Entries, reviews, comments, and other content you choose to create.
- **Tracker integration information:** When you connect AniList or MyAnimeList, the app may access information from those accounts, such as anime lists, status, and progress, to import or synchronize information into urAnime.
- **Social and sharing information:** Connection requests, accepted connections, and information you choose to share with other users through profile, library, or journal sharing features.
- **Notification information:** Device and push notification tokens used to register your device and deliver notifications and reminders.
- **Technical information:** Device and app information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the app.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and maintain urAnime's features.
- Manage accounts and authenticate users.
- Save anime-related preferences, library progress, and journal content.
- Import anime tracking information from connected AniList and MyAnimeList accounts.
- Enable connections and user-selected sharing features.
- Send notifications and reminders when enabled.
- Improve app functionality, reliability, and security.
- Respond to support requests.

## Third-Party Services

urAnime uses third-party services to provide certain features. These may include:

- **Supabase:** Account authentication, database storage, and backend services.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** Push notifications.
- **Resend:** Sending account-related emails.
- **AniList and MyAnimeList:** Connecting tracker accounts and importing anime tracking information when you choose to connect them.
- **Jikan and Kitsu:** Obtaining anime and episode metadata.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective features. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## Sharing and Visibility

Information you choose to share through profile, connection, or social features may be visible to other users according to the sharing settings available in the app.

When you connect an external anime tracker, urAnime accesses information from that tracker to provide the integration features you request.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the app's services, maintain account functionality, and meet applicable legal obligations.

When you delete your account, we initiate deletion of your account and associated personal information through the account deletion feature. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or operational records where necessary for security, legal, or operational purposes.

## Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your urAnime account and associated personal information through the account deletion feature in the app.

You can also contact us at:

**Email:** support@uranime.com

## Children's Privacy

urAnime is not intended for children under the age required to use the app under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by law.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
