# Delete Your urAnime Account

You can delete your urAnime account and associated personal data directly from the urAnime mobile app.

## How to delete your account

1. Open the urAnime app and sign in.
2. Navigate to your Profile.
3. Select the Delete Account option.
4. Follow the on-screen confirmation steps.

Once you confirm, urAnime processes the account deletion through its account deletion service.

## Can't access the app?

If you cannot access urAnime, email [support@uranime.com](mailto:support@uranime.com) from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion.

## What data is deleted?

Account deletion removes your account and associated user data, including profile information, library data, journal entries, social interactions, integrations, and notification-related data, as applicable.

Some data may remain temporarily in backups or operational records according to service providers' retention practices.
